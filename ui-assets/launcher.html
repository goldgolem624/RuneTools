Stop saving accounts?

The launcher will stop saving a session when you log into a client.

What this turns off:

  • New accounts you log into will not appear under Saved accounts.
  • Launch only works for accounts saved before now. Anything else has to be logged in through the game.
  • Opening a second client for another account means signing in again each time, rather than launching it from here.

Not affected: detecting running clients, showing their account names, and hiding a client's panel. You can turn this back on whenever you like.
